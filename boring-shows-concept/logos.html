Keep the deadpan ticket. Lose the noise.

Logo 1 has the right idea but is too busy. Logo 2 crosses out our own name, so it reads like a cancelled show. These are three cleaner options, tested at real app icon sizes.

Light and dark logos

Final direction

Light is a paper ticket: outlined, deadpan, with a lime shadow. Dark is a lit ticket: solid lime, glowing and awake. Each has its own face, so they read as day and night, not just a colour swap.
